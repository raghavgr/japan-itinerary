Arashiyama at Dawn · Bamboo Grove · Kinkaku-ji · Ryoan-ji · Tenzan Onsen 🛁

▼
Why Arashiyama first? The bamboo grove at 7AM is a completely different experience — silent, misty, green-gold light filtering through the canopy with virtually no one there. By 10AM it's a shoulder-to-shoulder parade. Kinkaku-ji is slightly later (10:30 vs 9AM opening) but still very manageable in the morning. This sequence makes both places dramatically better.
  • 06:30 — Depart for Arashiyama
    JR Sagano Line from Kyoto Station → Saga-Arashiyama 15min ¥240 ($1.65) First train of the day. Take a konbini coffee for the ride.
  • 07:00 — Bamboo Grove (Chikurin-no-Michi)
    Arashiyama itinerary bucket list details Virtually empty at this hour. Free. Towering culms of bamboo, morning mist, birds. The creaking sound of bamboo in the breeze is as memorable as the visuals. 15-min walk through the grove.
    📸 Face straight down the grove path at 7AM — no people, canopy of green-gold light overhead, disappearing into mist. This is the shot.
  • 07:45 — Togetsu-kyo Bridge + Oi River
    The iconic Arashiyama bridge with the forested mountain behind. Sakura trees line the river in late March. Best light before 8AM.
    📸 Togetsu-kyo bridge at dawn — mountain reflection in calm water, sakura on both banks.
  • 08:30 — Tenryu-ji Garden (opens 8:30AM)
    ¥500 ($3.40). UNESCO World Heritage Zen garden. Sakura in full bloom against the Arashiyama mountain backdrop — one of the most beautifully composed sakura views in Kyoto. The pond garden with karesansui (dry landscape) is serene at opening time.
  • 10:00 — Bus to Kinkaku-ji
    Bus 11 from Arashiyama → Kinkakuji area ~35min ¥230 ($1.55) or taxi ¥1,500 ($10.20).
  • 10:30 — Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion)
    ¥500 ($3.40). ~1.5hrs after opening — busier than ideal but still very manageable in the morning before lunch crowds.
    Ideally this is visited at 9AM sharp, but since Arashiyama bamboo grove at dawn is the higher priority, 10:30 is the trade-off. Still beautiful. Budget 30–40min max and leave before 11:30.
    📸 Golden pavilion reflection in the mirror pond. Shoot from the main viewing platform angle — frame cherry blossoms in the left foreground.
  • 11:30 — Ryoan-ji (Rock Garden)
    10-min walk from Kinkaku-ji. ¥600 ($4.10). The 15-rock Zen garden where you can only ever see 14 rocks at once from any angle. 30-min visit. Quieter than Kinkaku-ji.
  • 12:30 — Lunch
    Near Ryoan-ji/Kinkaku-ji area. Tofu kaiseki ¥1,500–2,500 ($10–17) or soba.
  • 14:00 — Return to Arashiyama for Tenzan Onsen 🛁
    Bus 11 reverse → Arashiyama ~35min ¥230 ($1.55) Arashiyama Tenzan Onsen (天山の湯): Natural mineral spring, outdoor rotenburo overlooking the Oi River. ¥800 ($5.45). Towel rental ¥200 ($1.35). 60–90min soak.

  • 16:30 — Return to Kyoto
    JR Sagano Line from Saga-Arashiyama → Kyoto Station 15min ¥240 ($1.65)

    Narrow lantern-lit alley along the Kamo River. Restaurants with kawayuka (river terrace) seating. ¥3,000–6,000/person ($20–41).

Itohkyuemon in Uji (30min from Kyoto, Kintetsu) for the widest range. Budget ¥3,000–10,000 total ($20–68). The in-store tea bar lets you taste before buying — ¥1,200 for the experience ($8.15).
